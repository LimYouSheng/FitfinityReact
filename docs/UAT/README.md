## Before starting

The organiser supplies the test website and prepared test accounts/records. Do not enter real customer details. Cases marked “with assistance” need someone to arrange a delay, interruption or special record. Mark them Blocked if they are not ready; do not change your device clock.

## Marking results

- **Pass:** every part matches the expected result.
- **Fail:** something differs; describe it in Remarks.
- **Blocked:** an account, record or assisted condition is not ready.
- **Not tested:** not attempted yet.

After a successful save, refresh and check again after signing in afresh. After Cancel or rejection, confirm the saved information is unchanged. For multi-part cases, record each attempt in Remarks; one failed part makes the case Fail.

Record noticeable slowness even if an action succeeds: what was slow, roughly how many seconds, first/repeat attempt and whether you could still cancel or navigate. Do not put passwords, verification codes or real health details in remarks/screenshots.

Case IDs stay the same across role worksheets so shared findings can be compared. Role-specific actions differ intentionally. Mark only the role/device you actually tested. Keep failed results and record the later retest separately.
